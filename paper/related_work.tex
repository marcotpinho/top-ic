\subsection{Rewarded routing and continuous motion}
The orienteering problem selects rewarded vertices under a route budget~\cite{golden1987orienteering}; the team variant distributes that selection across several routes while crediting a task once~\cite{chao1996team}. The literature includes exact and heuristic methods for time windows, clustered rewards, and other operational restrictions~\cite{gunawan2016survey}. Multi-robot environmental monitoring is one concrete use of this allocation model~\cite{mansfield2021multirobot}. Kinematic orienteering replaces a distance surrogate with time-optimal connections under velocity and acceleration limits and considers both mathematical programming and large-neighborhood search~\cite{meyer2022kop}. We build on the visit-state representation of kinematic orienteering, extend reward accounting to a team, and couple the resulting continuous trajectories through temporal communication.

\subsection{Connectivity-aware team routing}
Continuous-connectivity methods maintain a connected communication graph during motion, using centralized or decentralized control and, more recently, policies that account for uncertainty~\cite{yang2010decentralized,li2021decentralized,shetty2023decentralized}. Such guarantees provide an information path at every instant, but they can force a team to remain geographically compact. TOP-CC is closer to our routing objective: it combines team orienteering with communication criteria in a multi-objective search~\cite{topcc}. We allow agents to disconnect, measure the age of propagated information rather than instantaneous graph quality, and charge continuous kinematic flight time; consequently, the TOP-CC results are not a matched numerical baseline for our model.

\subsection{Intermittent communication and rendezvous}
Intermittent-connectivity planning coordinates motion and scheduled communication events, including temporal-logic missions and exploration under information-consistency requirements~\cite{kantaros2016intermittent,kantaros2017temporal,klaesson2019intermittent}. Recent rendezvous work schedules temporary meetings while robots explore under limited communication~\cite{silva2023rendezvous}. These methods establish that temporary separation can be compatible with team-level information requirements. Our formulation differs by evaluating every range contact induced by acceleration-constrained reward routes and by bounding directed information age through contacts in chronological order.

\subsection{Multi-objective neighborhood search}
Variable-neighborhood search systematically changes move structures to escape local optima, and its multi-objective variants maintain a set of nondominated alternatives~\cite{hansen2017vns,duarte2015movns}. Orienteering heuristics commonly combine constructive seeds, assignment or ordering moves, and large perturbations~\cite{gunawan2016survey}. We use this framework but separate feasible reward--time solutions from valid communication-infeasible stepping stones, so partial communication repair can guide the search without becoming a reported objective.
