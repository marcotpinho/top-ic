# Team KOP experiments

This stage tests MOVNS with two agents, shared reward, independent elapsed-time budgets, acceleration-aware motion, and an epsilon-box archive. Collisions are ignored. Communication and free waypoints belong to step 4 and are not implemented here.

## Reproduce

From the branch checkout, create an isolated environment with `uv venv .venv` and `uv pip install --python .venv/bin/python -e '.[test]'`. Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/benchmark_team.py` in a fresh output directory. The script deliberately refuses an existing campaign ledger: the recorded campaign must not be overwritten or silently rerun. Use `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q` for correctness checks.

The archive minimizes `(-unique_reward, mean_completion_time)`, with initial epsilon widths 1 reward unit and 0.1 seconds. If its capacity requires coarsening, effective widths are logged and independently validated. Delta is not a Pareto objective and is not evaluated in this stage.

## Benchmark provenance

The files in [benchmarks/tsiligirides](../benchmarks/tsiligirides/) preserve the authentic KU Leuven Tsiligirides OP2 download, its budget-30 text instance, and provenance/checksums. The team conversion preserves all 19 task coordinates and rewards, interpreting coordinates as meters. Two agents receive **new 20-second budgets**, componentwise velocity bounds of 3 m/s and acceleration bounds of 1.5 m/s², and zero initial/final velocities. The original geometric-distance budget is not reinterpreted as seconds. These are adapted KOP experiments; published OP optimal values are not comparable.

Budget includes motion and en-route waiting until individual arrival. Post-arrival presence is free. Reward includes incidental continuous passage and is counted once across agents.

## Protocol

The final campaign contains full seed 42, six singleton ablations (insert, remove, replace, reorder, velocity, assignment), and full seeds 7 and 99. Every optimizer subprocess has a maximum of 4,000 evaluations, 100 iterations, a 45-second soft deadline, and a 120-second hard process timeout. Search stops at whichever bound is reached first. The wall-clock budget means actual evaluation counts can differ; comparisons are descriptive, not paired equal-evaluation evidence.

One earlier crowding-archive full run was interrupted before completing when epsilon dominance was requested. Its start remains in [superseded-ledger.json](../experiments/team/superseded-ledger.json). Together with the nine epsilon starts this is **10 total optimizer starts**, with no retries. Unit and plot correctness tests are separate checks.

For every saved front, [validate_team.py](../scripts/validate_team.py) checks polynomial dynamics, continuity, component limits, endpoint velocity/position, individual completion budgets, continuous task passage, shared reward, first visitors, objective values, ordinary nondominance, and epsilon-box nondominance. It does not call the production trajectory connector or evaluator. Raw JSON stores complete trajectories and routes; ledgers store validation residuals and timings; the manifest hashes outputs.

## Results

| Variant | Max reward | Front points | Evaluations | Runtime (s) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| full | 170 | 14 | 3524 | 45.002 |
| without-insert | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0.015 |
| without-remove | 170 | 15 | 3491 | 45.008 |
| without-replace | 185 | 14 | 3515 | 45.000 |
| without-reorder | 170 | 14 | 3313 | 45.002 |
| without-velocity | 170 | 14 | 3263 | 45.019 |
| without-assignment | 160 | 13 | 3145 | 45.017 |
| full-seed7 | 165 | 14 | 2939 | 45.002 |
| full-seed99 | 210 | 14 | 3513 | 45.016 |

All nine final runs passed independent validation. No archive coarsening occurred (effective widths remained 1 reward unit / 0.1 seconds). Full-run maximum rewards ranged from 165 to 210 across seeds 7, 42, and 99. Removing replacement improved the seed-42 reward to 185, while removing assignment reduced it to 160. These differences cannot establish general neighborhood rankings.

Correctness suite: **27 tests passed**. Maximum saved-trajectory validation residual: 4.94e-08 (tolerance 2e-6). Nine completed runs were below 46 seconds each; the one interrupted superseded run also remained below the 120-second bound.

These short runs on one adapted instance are regression and feasibility evidence. Disabling insert also disables constructive insertion initialization, leaving the direct route unable to acquire tasks. A singleton ablation changes construction and subsequent search behavior; its outcome is not a causal estimate of a neighborhood's general value. Three full seeds and one seed per ablation are insufficient for statistical claims or a comparison against external KOP algorithms.
